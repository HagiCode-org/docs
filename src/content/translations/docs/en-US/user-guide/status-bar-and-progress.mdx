The bottom edge of the workbench and a few widgets at the top keep you informed without getting in the way. This page covers the status bar, the trophies and achievement hall, and your profile.

## Status bar

![Status bar with the Microsoft Store badge, a rotating tip, Agent CLI health, running sessions, break reminder, salary tracker, and version](../../img/user-guide/workbench/04-status-bar.png)

From left to right:

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Microsoft Store badge** | A link to the HagiCode listing in the Microsoft Store. |
| **Rotating tips and promotions** | One tip or promotion at a time, scrolling. Tips explain how to use HagiCode; promotions link to related projects. You can turn the scroll off or choose its sources in **Settings → Integration → Status Bar Scroll**. |
| **Agent CLI health** | Small badges such as `CC`, `CO`, and `GC` for Claude Code, Codex, and GitHub Copilot, each with a green dot when healthy. `+N` counts additional tools. Click the badges to refresh the health check. |
| **Running sessions** | The number of sessions running now. Click it for details. |
| **Break reminder** | A drop icon that shows how much of the current reminder interval remains. |
| **Salary tracker** | The unit emoji and today's accumulated amount. Shown while the tracker and its status bar indicator are on. |
| **Version** | The build version, for example `dev` in a development build. |

### Running sessions

![Running sessions popover with the session limit, the overclock option, and no running sessions](../../img/user-guide/status/03-running-sessions.png)

HagiCode limits how many sessions run at the same time, 20 by default. The popover shows the current count against the limit and lists running sessions.

- **Overclock** raises the limit by 3. It becomes available once you reach the current limit; the popover shows how many times it has been used.
- The TurboEngine DLC can raise the concurrency tier further.

### Break reminder

![Break reminder popover with a message and Remind me later and Take a break buttons](../../img/user-guide/status/04-break-reminder.png)

When the timer fires, a card appears with the message of the active reminder theme. **Take a break** acknowledges it and **Remind me later** snoozes it. Configure the interval and tone in **Settings → Integration → Break Reminder**.

### Salary tracker

![Salary tracker popover with per-minute rate, today, elapsed minutes, yesterday, and historical total](../../img/user-guide/status/05-salary-tracker.png)

Click the emoji to see the per-minute rate, today's total, elapsed minutes, yesterday's total, and the historical total. **Open settings** jumps to **Settings → Integration → Salary Tracker**, where you set the daily salary and work hours.

## Trophies and the Achievement Hall

The **Trophies** widget at the top right of the session board shows your progress as stars. Four stars make a moon, four moons make a sun, and four suns make a crown. The number beside the title shows how many stars away the next promotion is.

Click it to open the **Achievement Hall**.

![Achievement Hall with daily score, completion rate, today's raid overview, and online growth](../../img/user-guide/status/01-achievements.png)

- **Daily Achievements** are goals that reset each day, such as starting and finishing proposals, agent output, and tool activity. The header shows the daily score, for example `0/17`.
- **Global Achievements** are lifetime milestones that accumulate across days.
- The **Mission Board** summarizes the day: completion rate, active goals, and the reward lane.
- **Online growth** tracks progress that accrues while HagiCode is running.

Points you earn here and from daily work appear in your profile. Daily progress popovers can be switched off in **Settings → Game → Achievements**.

## Profile

Click the round avatar at the far right of the header to open your profile drawer.

![Profile drawer with the avatar, points, a history timeline, and quick links](../../img/user-guide/status/02-profile.png)

- **Avatar** – upload a JPEG, PNG, WEBP, or GIF image up to 10 MB.
- **Points** – your current settled balance, earned from achievements and daily work.
- **History timeline** – recent system history such as sessions created or deleted. It loads only when the drawer opens.
- **Quick links** – for example **View Yesterday's Report**, which opens the full-screen hero battle report for yesterday.
