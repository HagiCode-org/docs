## What This Section Covers

The FAQ section is the place for:

- startup and runtime caveats
- synchronized usage tips that should stay available as a stable article
- compatibility notes tied to platforms, distribution channels, packaging, or bundled runtimes
- troubleshooting topics that deserve a stable standalone explanation page

If you want a stable article version of HagiCode daily guidance, open [HagiCode Usage Tips](/en-US/faq/tips). If you need to confirm which Node Desktop uses, what to do when Node cannot be found, or why diagnostics show `portable-fixed/toolchain` after an upgrade, open [How Desktop Uses Your Node Environment](/en-US/faq/desktop-node-environment). If you want to know why HagiCode is not on Steam, open [Why HagiCode is not available on Steam](/en-US/faq/steam-distribution-status).
