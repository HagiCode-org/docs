## What this section covers

The FAQ section collects the following:

- Special notes about startup and the runtime
- Usage tips from Index that are worth keeping as long-lived articles
- Compatibility notes related to platforms, distribution channels, packaging, or bundled runtimes
- Common troubleshooting entry points that deserve a standalone explanation page
