## Why you need an LLM guide

In code generation scenarios, models differ noticeably in stability, speed, code quality, and cost. This category provides a consistent comparison framework to help you choose a model quickly at different stages of a project.

## Recommended reading path

1. Start with [Model Comparison and Evaluation in HagiCode](./model-comparison-evaluation/) to learn the comparison dimensions.
2. Pick candidate models based on your project goal (quality first, cost first, or speed first).
3. Use the update dates and scenario notes in the article to confirm that its conclusions still apply to your task.

## Maintenance principles

- Update the evaluation conclusions whenever model capabilities change noticeably.
- Re-check the test date and the scope of the conclusions at least once a month.
- Keep the Chinese and English pages aligned in section structure.
